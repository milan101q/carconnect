Questions dealers ask.

What do you need from me to get started?

Just your dealership or auction website where your current inventory is listed. We use it to build your Facebook Marketplace listings, so you don't need to send photos or descriptions separately.

Who do buyers contact?

You. Interested buyers reach out to you directly, so there's no middleman between you and your customer.

How often are my vehicles posted?

Each order covers one month, during which your inventory is posted to Facebook Marketplace every week, and 10% of your payment is used to boost your vehicles for extra reach.

Is this a monthly subscription?

No. We don't lock you into a subscription or a contract. You pay once per order, and whenever you want your inventory posted again, just place a new order.

What if I have more than 70 vehicles?
